Settings → Displays → Hide offers a "Remove from desktop" toggle; off means Hide blacks the display out, which the toggle does not say. The user asked for Hide "with mirroring disabled (like just blackout)" from that section. Replace the toggle with one choice per display: Black out (default), Remove from desktop (mirror onto a chosen display, optional input switch), and Turn off once DP-100 lands. Existing saved configurations keep their behavior: enabled=true is Remove from desktop, otherwise Black out.
