Goal: turn off one external monitor (real standby, not a black window) while the other displays stay on, then turn it back on from the Mac. This differs from handoff (DP-87): no other computer is involved, and macOS reconnecting the display on wake or after a crash only turns the monitor back on, which is harmless. Blackout leaves the monitor powered; QD-OLED panels such as AW3425DW may run pixel refresh only in standby. sleep-displays sleeps every display.

Candidates:
- Hide to an unused input. With Auto Select Off the monitor sees no signal and enters standby. No private API or power command. Show must switch it back over DDC from standby, which some monitors refuse. Does not work with Auto Select On: the monitor scans and returns to the Mac's mirror.
- Session-long private disconnect that accepts macOS reconnects. Works with Auto Select On because the Mac's signal is gone. The existing helper already reconnects on app exit; the change is lifting the 1–60 s cap (15 s in the app) and possibly multiple targets. DP-9's 15-second S2721DGF cycle lost Mac video on the monitor, but standby and electrical link shutdown were not confirmed.
- DDC power (VCP 0xD6, CLI-only `dp ddc-power`). Independent of inputs, but monitor firmware decides what Off means and some monitors stop answering DDC or need power removal.

Depends on the DP-87 unused-input standby observation. AW3423DW is out of scope: its DDC reads fail. Safety: every private setter call, DDC write or live restoration needs fresh scoped approval of exact target, bounded duration, usable survivor, user presence and fallback. No permanent configuration, relaxed identity checks or discarded journals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For AW3425DW, S2721DGF and K272HUL, record per candidate whether the monitor reaches real standby, whether the Mac turns it back on without the monitor buttons, and behavior on app quit or crash, Mac sleep/wake and reboot. Mark each case observed or untested; no live write without fresh scoped approval.
- [ ] Recommend one candidate per monitor, or reject, with reasons and limits, and say whether it needs Auto Select Off. Compare with Blackout and sleep-displays.
- [ ] If a candidate is recommended, file scoped follow-up implementation work with its safety contract; otherwise record why each candidate was rejected.
